What this dashboard is for

Rohit AI Studio brings customer conversations and everyday business follow-up into one workspace. It combines a customer list, lead pipeline, inbox, bookings, offer catalog, AI configuration, automations, and communication tools.

The important difference: Trial and Live

TRIAL is a safe practice environment. Messages and calls are simulated inside the app; Trial AI is a local preview. Nothing is sent to a real customer's WhatsApp, phone, SMS, or inbox, and no paid AI provider is needed.

LIVE sends through real, configured providers for that organization. Only an authorized platform super-admin can activate it, after confirming setup and plan status. A missing or failing real provider is an error—not a pretend success.

One workspace at a time

Each business has its own contacts, leads, conversations, offers, settings, Trial/Live mode, and provider sender details.

One practical routine

Set up business facts, add offers and contacts, practice a conversation, record opportunities, and review the next action.

Current product boundary: Trial is for learning the screens and message flow, not for verifying a real-world AI booking or contacting actual customers. Some advanced actions, especially AI tool-based booking, require a correctly configured Live AI workflow.

This guide describes the current implementation. It does not promise online checkout, automatic billing, or that every listed provider is already connected.

Customer operations

Contacts, leads & conversations

Contacts: your customer record

  1. Open Contacts and choose Add contact.
  2. Enter the customer's name and the phone/email address you will use. Add a company and useful tags if relevant.
  3. Save, then use this record in channel messaging, the lead pipeline, or a conversation.

Leads: a possible sale that needs follow-up

Create a lead from an existing contact, then move it through the pipeline as the relationship progresses. Add truthful source/value details and update the stage after real conversations.

Example stages: New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Won (or Lost). “Won” is your team’s tracking status; it is not a payment processor.

Conversation desk: the customer thread

  • Open a thread to review its messages and contact details.
  • Use Take over when a human should handle the issue; AI should stop talking over an escalated conversation.
  • Use Reopen AI to hand an open thread back, or resolve it when finished.
  • In Trial, a reply is stored as a demo and is not delivered externally. In Live, it uses the configured channel provider.
Privacy habit: add only information your team needs. Workspace records are scoped so users see data only in workspaces where they have active access.
Teach the system

Offers, AI agents & automations

1 · Add accurate offers

In Offer library, add the real service name, price, duration, and a short description. The AI uses active offers as business facts. Keep prices and policies up to date; do not expect it to know facts you have not supplied.

2 · Give an agent a clear job

In AI agents, create an agent and write instructions about role, tone, service facts, boundaries, and when to escalate. Set one active agent as default. Be explicit: “Do not promise a slot until availability is checked. Escalate billing disputes to a human.”

3 · Automate repeatable follow-up

In Automation engine, choose a trigger, optional condition, and action. Current triggers include lead created, customer message received, and appointment booked. Actions include sending a WhatsApp/SMS/email message or updating a lead stage. Templates can use data such as {{contact.name}}.

After an event fires, inspect Recent runs. Failed runs show an error; do not assume an automation worked just because it was saved.

Trial note: Trial AI replies are local previews and don't call the external AI or booking tools. Use Trial to learn the interface and test demo message recording. A confirmed AI booking or live follow-up depends on Live provider setup and a supported workflow.
Safe practice

What happens in Trial mode?

Trial mode lets you practice without paying for or configuring communication APIs. It records demo activity in the workspace so you can see how the interface behaves.

Default trial controls

Current defaults are configured centrally and can be adjusted by a super-admin: 14 days, up to 100 WhatsApp demo messages, 100 SMS demos, 50 email demos, 30 voice demos, and 3 users. Enabled feature areas can also be configured per workspace.

The control panel can change trial limits. Automatic payment, plan renewal, and automated Trial-to-Live conversion are not implemented.

Example business

Real-life example: a fitness studio

Imagine “UrbanFit Studio” receives enquiries about personal training. The owner wants to avoid missing interested prospects and keep a consistent record of each conversation.

1

Set up facts first

In Business setup, enter UrbanFit's name, hours, location, and rules. Write accurate AI instructions: session length, prices, who can book, cancellation policy, and when to ask a human.

2

Add the service

In Offer library, add “Personal training consultation”, ₹1,500, 60 minutes, and a clear description. Verify this is the current price.

3

Practice safely

Use the Trial WhatsApp simulation (for example, from the Overview card or the Conversation desk) with a fictional name and number. Enter: “Hi, I want to know about personal training.” The conversation and demo response appear in the inbox. No WhatsApp is sent.

4

Record the opportunity

Add the fictional person as a contact. Create a lead for that contact and move it from New to Contacted after your team follows up. Use the conversation desk to practice a human reply or escalation.

5

Review what needs attention

Use Overview for new leads/conversations; scan the lead pipeline; resolve a thread only when follow-up is complete. Review automation runs for failures.

What this practice proves: your team can navigate the dashboard, store a customer journey, test demo messaging, and agree on a follow-up process. It does not prove real delivery, provider connectivity, payment processing, or automatic booking.
From demo to real customer operations

Live activation: admin checklist

Do not switch a workspace to Live simply to make the indicator green. Live mode can send real messages or place calls. Verify the business has approved the setup and the exact providers first.

  1. Prepare the real provider setup. A deployment administrator configures provider secrets securely in the backend environment. Each workspace also needs its own sender ID/number/address in the admin setup.
  2. Verify a plan. Payment is currently collected outside the application. A super-admin records the applicable plan after manually confirming payment.
  3. Review readiness. At least one real communication channel and a real AI provider must be configured. Test the sender in a safe, authorized way.
  4. Confirm activation. In Business setup → Trial → Live activation, select the correct workspace, confirm the plan/provider setup, choose Live, and type the required confirmation phrase.
  5. Start small. Have an authorized team member verify a permitted test recipient, channel, and message. Check its delivery status and provider result before expanding.
  6. Monitor and respond. Check the inbox, failed delivery status, automation run history, and customer escalation. Human staff remain responsible for customer care.

Only a platform super-admin can change modes

Normal workspace owners/admins cannot activate their own workspace. The super-admin email must be listed in the backend's SUPER_ADMIN_EMAILS. Mode changes require explicit confirmation and are audit logged with previous mode, new mode, actor, and time.

Current limitations: payment is manual; there is no checkout flow. Provider API secrets are shared deployment environment settings, not encrypted per-customer credentials. Multiple sender identities per channel and automated subscription renewal are future work. If a Live provider is missing or fails, the system should show failure rather than silently simulate.
Good daily habits

A simple operating routine

Start of day

  • Open Overview and look for conversations/leads needing attention.
  • Check upcoming appointments.
  • Assign clear ownership for follow-up.

During the day

  • Add new contacts accurately.
  • Update lead stage after a real customer interaction.
  • Take over conversations requiring a human.

Before using AI

  • Review prices and services in Offer library.
  • Keep hours, policies, and escalation instructions current.
  • Review agent instructions with your team.

Before Live sends

  • Confirm workspace shows LIVE.
  • Check provider readiness and recipient details.
  • Inspect failed statuses and automation runs; correct problems promptly.
